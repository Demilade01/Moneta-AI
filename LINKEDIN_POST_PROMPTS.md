# LinkedIn Post Prompts for Moneta AI

Use these prompts with Gemini AI to generate engaging LinkedIn posts about your Moneta AI project. You can use the screenshots you have along with these prompts.

---

## Prompt Option 1: Technical/Developer-Focused Post

```
I'm building Moneta AI, an AI-powered pricing intelligence platform. Here's what it does:

**Project Overview:**
- Name: Moneta AI - "Your Intelligent Pricing Analyst"
- Tagline: "Make confident, data-driven pricing decisions with AI-powered analysis, simulations, and explainable recommendations. No black boxes. Just clarity."

**Key Features:**
1. Multi-Agent AI System - 4 specialized AI agents (Data Analyst, Market Analyst, Pricing Strategist, Recommendation Agent) working together using LangGraph
2. GPT-4 Powered - Advanced reasoning with OpenAI GPT-4o for intelligent pricing decisions
3. Pricing Analytics Engine - Price elasticity, competitor analysis, revenue trends, and margin optimization
4. Smart Recommendations - AI-generated pricing suggestions with confidence scores, impact projections, and clear reasoning
5. Interactive Dashboards - Real-time visualizations for revenue trends, category performance, and competitor comparison
6. Price Simulations - Test pricing strategies before implementation with accurate revenue forecasting
7. Data Upload System - Drag-and-drop CSV/Excel files with automatic column mapping
8. Explainable AI - Full transparency with detailed reasoning for every recommendation

**Tech Stack:**
- Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19
- TailwindCSS 4, shadcn/ui, Radix UI
- Framer Motion + GSAP for animations
- LangChain, LangGraph, OpenAI GPT-4o
- tRPC for type-safe APIs
- Prisma ORM + PostgreSQL
- Recharts for data visualization

**Design:**
Premium glassmorphism design with pure black & white color palette, semi-transparent glass cards, and smooth animations.

Write a professional LinkedIn post (2-3 paragraphs) that:
- Highlights the problem it solves (pricing decisions are complex and risky)
- Emphasizes the AI-powered approach and explainability
- Mentions the tech stack briefly
- Includes a call-to-action to check it out
- Uses an engaging, professional tone suitable for developers and tech professionals
- Keeps it concise but informative
- Add relevant hashtags at the end
```

## Tips for Using These Prompts:

1. **Choose the style** that matches your LinkedIn audience (technical vs. business-focused)
2. **Add your screenshots** when using the prompt - mention "I have screenshots showing [describe what's in screenshots]"
3. **Customize** - Add your own personal touches, challenges you faced, or lessons learned
4. **Hashtags** - Ask Gemini to suggest relevant hashtags, or use: #AI #PricingIntelligence #SaaS #NextJS #OpenAI #DataAnalytics #BusinessIntelligence #TechInnovation
5. **Call-to-Action** - Include a link to your live demo or ask for feedback
6. **Engagement** - Ask a question at the end to encourage comments
